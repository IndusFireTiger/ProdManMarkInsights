## The problem

AI governance moved from principle to obligation quickly. Teams now need to
know which laws and standards apply to them, what "responsible AI" means in
practice, and how to run governance as part of the work rather than as
paperwork. Most of the material is either too abstract to act on or locked
behind paid courses.

## What it does

- **A structured curriculum.** 38 lessons in seven modules: Foundations,
  Responsible AI, the Regulatory Landscape, Frameworks & Standards,
  Operationalising Governance, Assurance & GenAI Risk, and an AI Risk
  Catalogue. Each lesson ends in a quiz.
- **The Advisor.** Five questions about your organisation return the laws,
  standards and next steps most likely to apply.
- **The Atlas.** A worldwide directory of AI laws, frameworks and standards,
  filterable by region, type and scope.
- **News.** A curated feed of AI governance developments, newest first, so
  the curriculum stays connected to what's changing.

## Product choices worth noting

- **Sequenced, not a library.** Modules run from foundations to
  operationalising governance and assurance, so a learner builds up to the
  hard parts instead of dipping in at random.
- **Free, with no sign-in.** Progress lives in the browser. Nothing stands
  between a curious reader and the first lesson.
- **Learn, then apply.** The Advisor and the Atlas turn the curriculum into
  answers about your own organisation, the same pattern as the
  [DRC Academy](/work/drc-academy) Navigator, reused for a new domain.
- **A living product.** The news feed keeps it current in a field that
  changes monthly.
