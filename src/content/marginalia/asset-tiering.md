Treating a minor enhancement and a flagship capability with the same launch
machinery wastes effort on one and starves the other. Tiering fixes the bundle in
advance against a trigger — new strategic capability, meaningful enhancement,
incremental fix — so the only per-launch decision is *which tier is this?*

The saving isn't mainly in production. It's in the argument that no longer
happens: the recurring negotiation about how much a given release deserves,
which in most organisations is settled by whoever advocates loudest rather than
by what the market needs.
