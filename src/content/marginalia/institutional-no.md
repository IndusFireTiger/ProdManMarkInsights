Competitive analysis is built around rival products, because rivals are visible,
nameable and have websites you can screenshot. Meanwhile most opportunities are
lost to something with no logo: the institution declining to act.

The asymmetry is structural. Choosing your product requires a budget line, a
security review, a procurement process, a migration, retraining, and a person
willing to attach their name to it. Choosing nothing requires none of those and
has never got anyone fired. Any honest read of the
[alternatives](/marginalia/competitive-alternative) has to weight it accordingly.

The practical consequence for messaging: pointing the pitch at a rival's feature
list argues about the wrong comparison. What moves a stalled deal is aiming at
the structural pain the organisation already admits it has, and lowering the
perceived risk of acting — references from institutions like them, a smaller
first commitment, an integration into the systems they already run.

Win by being the relief, not the risk.
