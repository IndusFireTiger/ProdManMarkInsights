The claim is narrower than it sounds. Agents don't make a product marketer
smarter and they have no taste. What they remove is the reason the upstream seat
was unaffordable: the labour — trawling win/loss calls, diffing competitor
releases, rebuilding battlecards, cutting the fourth persona's one-pager — that
consumes the calendar before judgement work begins.

The governance line is the whole design: **agents draft, gather, correlate and
monitor; humans decide, approve, price and defend.** Every artifact carries an
agent's byline and a human's signature, and the signature is what makes it real.

But the agents aren't the intervention. The **gate** is — no roadmap item gets
full budget until a positioning artifact exists and has been signed. Gates like
that have been recommended for years and almost never held, because a gate
costing three days of a stretched person's week gets waived under deadline. What
agents change is the economics of holding it.

See it drawn on a real artifact in the [FitPulse pricing
teardown](/work/fitpulse-pricing-agent), where the agent's judgement visibly runs
out.
