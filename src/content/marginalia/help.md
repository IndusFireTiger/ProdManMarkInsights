## Higher Education Loan Program

The Australian government scheme under which eligible students defer tuition and
repay through the tax system once income passes a threshold. HECS-HELP is the
best-known component; several others cover full-fee, postgraduate and support
loans.

It matters to product work in the sector because it isn't only a funding
mechanism — it's a **reporting obligation**. Eligibility, census dates, unit
enrolment and withdrawal all have to be captured precisely and reported to
government, which is a large part of why the [student management
system](/marginalia/sms) is so difficult to replace. The compliance surface is
statutory, dated and unforgiving.

**Not the ordinary word "help."** Always the loan program in this writing.
