The launch factory is a *symptom*, not a disease. It's what happens at a
PMM-to-PM ratio of 1:5 or worse: collateral has a date on it and discovery
doesn't, so collateral wins every week, permanently.

Which sets the trap. Agents and automation are very good at producing launch
collateral, so the obvious first move is to point them there. Lead times
collapse, someone puts "70% faster" on a slide, and product marketing remains
exactly as downstream as before — only now the visible pain that early
involvement was supposed to relieve has stopped itching.

Note also what that number measures: output volume. It looks identical whether
you fixed the problem or entrenched it, so it cannot distinguish between them.

The distinction from the [GTM Factory](/marginalia/gtm-factory) is the direction
of travel. The Factory is a system for producing launches well. The launch
factory is a queue producing launches regardless.
