## Heart rate

*The meaning used in the pricing case study.* Continuous heart-rate data from a
wearable, synced into a fitness product. It matters to that analysis for a
specific reason: it's the input the AI plan generator needs in order to
personalise anything, which is what makes bundling the two a decision grounded in
product logic rather than in packaging convenience.

It's also the source of the one genuine risk in that teardown — 60% of users
already had it for free, so gating it is a withdrawal rather than an upsell.

---

**Not human resources.** The far more common expansion in a business context, and
not the sense used anywhere in this writing.
