## Student Management System

The system of record for the student lifecycle: admissions, enrolment,
progression, results, graduation, and the statutory reporting that follows from
all of it. Also called a Student Information System (SIS) in North America.

It is the hardest product in the higher-education sector to build and the
riskiest to replace — it touches every process in the institution, carries
decades of accumulated local rules, and cannot be taken offline during a census
period. Which is why replacement programmes are measured in years and why the
incumbent vendors are so durable.

---

**Not text messaging.** In telecommunications SMS is Short Message Service, and
that's the meaning most search traffic intends. It never carries that sense in
this writing.
